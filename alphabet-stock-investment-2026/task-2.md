# Task 2: Alphabet's Current Financials and 2026 Earnings Outlook

## Current Financial Performance (Fiscal Year 2024)

Alphabet delivered strong financial results for fiscal year 2024, with total consolidated revenues of $350.0 billion, representing 14% year-over-year growth. The company achieved a 32% operating margin—a 5 percentage point expansion from the prior year—with net income of $100.1 billion and diluted earnings per share (EPS) of $8.04, up 31% annually. Key business segments demonstrated robust performance: Google Services revenues reached $307.4 billion with 10% year-over-year growth, while Google Cloud revenues surged 30% to $48.4 billion, driven by growth in Google Cloud Platform and generative AI solutions. In the fourth quarter of 2024 alone, consolidated revenues were $96.5 billion (12% growth), with operating income increasing 31% to $30.97 billion and EPS reaching $2.15.

## Revenue and Profitability Drivers

Google Services, which includes Search, YouTube, and subscriptions/devices, generated the majority of Alphabet's revenue base and continues to drive profitability. Google Search & other revenue increased 12% in Q4 2024 to $54.0 billion, while YouTube advertising grew 14% to $10.5 billion. The business model benefits from operational leverage, as demonstrated by the 32% operating margin achieved in 2024—up substantially from prior periods—indicating strong cost control and high-margin advertising revenue. Google Cloud's 30% annual revenue growth demonstrates successful monetization of AI infrastructure and platform services, though this segment currently operates at lower margins than core Search and YouTube. The operating income across Google Services improved to $32.8 billion in Q4 2024 from $26.7 billion in Q4 2023, driven by revenue growth and operational efficiency gains.

## 2026 Earnings Estimates and Capital Investment Plans

Analyst consensus forecasts a 7% growth in Alphabet's diluted earnings per share for 2026, representing a significant deceleration from the 33.3% growth achieved in 2025, yet still reflecting solid operational performance driven by the strength of digital advertising and Google Cloud momentum. Alphabet has announced ambitious capital expenditure plans of $75 billion for 2025, escalating to a projected range of $175–$185 billion in 2026 to support its AI infrastructure and data center expansion strategy. These substantial CapEx investments will pressure near-term profits through increased depreciation expenses and operational costs, though they are intended to support long-term AI competitive positioning and Google Cloud growth. Despite the earnings growth moderation forecast for 2026, analysts maintain a constructive outlook on valuation expansion potential, with consensus P/E ratio expectations rising from a current 28 to approximately 30 by end-of-2026, supported by the company's proven ability to monetize AI capabilities within its core Search and Cloud businesses.
