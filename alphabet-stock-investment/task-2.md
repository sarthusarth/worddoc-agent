# Task 2: Alphabet's Latest Financial Metrics

## Revenue and Growth Performance

Alphabet reported full-year 2024 revenues of $350.0 billion, representing 14% year-over-year growth (15% in constant currency). Notably, the company demonstrated strong momentum across all major business segments. Google Services generated $307.4 billion in annual revenue with 10% year-over-year growth in constant currency, driven by robust performance in Google Search & Other ($225.5 billion annually) and YouTube Advertising ($42.0 billion annually). Google Cloud emerged as a high-growth engine with $47.8 billion in annual revenue, up 30% year-over-year, benefiting from strong demand for Google Cloud Platform (GCP), AI Infrastructure, and Generative AI Solutions. In the fourth quarter specifically, consolidated Alphabet revenues reached $96.5 billion, up 12% year-over-year from $86.3 billion, demonstrating sustained growth momentum heading into 2025.

## Profitability Margins and Earnings Per Share

Alphabet's profitability metrics reveal strong operational efficiency and margin expansion. Operating income for the full year 2024 reached $112.4 billion on revenues of $350.0 billion, translating to an operating margin of 32%—representing a significant 500 basis point expansion compared to the 27% operating margin in 2023. This margin improvement reflects the company's ability to scale operations and leverage its AI-powered portfolio while managing costs effectively. Net income for 2024 totaled $100.1 billion, up 28% from $73.8 billion in 2023. Diluted earnings per share (EPS) increased substantially to $8.04 for the full year 2024, compared to $5.80 in 2023, representing a 31% increase year-over-year. In the fourth quarter alone, diluted EPS reached $2.15, up from $1.64 in Q4 2023.

## Capital Expenditure and Investment Strategy

While not specifically a debt metric, Alphabet's capital expenditure strategy is material to understanding the company's financial position and leverage. Management indicated in the Q4 2024 earnings announcement that the company expects to invest approximately $75 billion in capital expenditures during 2025, a significant increase reflecting the company's commitment to AI infrastructure and compute capacity. This substantial CapEx commitment demonstrates management's confidence in growth opportunities, particularly in AI-powered products and services, though it represents a notable increase from prior periods and will influence free cash flow generation going forward.

## Balance Sheet Strength and Financial Position

Alphabet maintains a strong financial position with the resources to fund aggressive capital expenditure programs. As of December 31, 2024, Alphabet's market capitalization exceeded $2.0 trillion (based on June 2024 measurement), positioning the company among the world's most valuable enterprises. The company's strong cash generation from operations, combined with its access to capital markets, provides substantial financial flexibility. While specific long-term debt figures are embedded in the company's 10-K filing, Alphabet has historically maintained a relatively conservative balance sheet with investment-grade credit ratings, enabling it to finance growth initiatives through both operating cash flows and capital markets access. The company's net income of $100.1 billion in 2024 and improved operating margins provide significant resources for both returning capital to shareholders (evidenced by $2.4 billion in dividend payments in Q4 alone) and investing in future growth.
